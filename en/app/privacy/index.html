Spine app – privacy

Applies to Spine for Android (industries.spine.app). Last changed 30 October 2026. Svenska

The Spine app is an alarm and control client for a Spine system. It is published by Ampano Technologies AB, Sundsvall, Sweden. It connects to a Spine server owned and operated by the organisation you use the app for, for example a property owner or an alarm operator. That organisation is responsible for the data on its server and for how long it is kept.

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no trackers. No data is sold or shared for marketing.

Sent to your organisation's Spine server

  • Sign-in with user name and password, a passkey or your fingerprint. Your fingerprint never leaves the phone; the phone only sends a signature made with a key held in the phone's secure storage.
  • Phone registration: a random ID for the phone, manufacturer and model, app version, the phone's public keys and its push address (an address from Google that notifications are sent to). An administrator approves the phone before it receives notifications.
  • What you do in the app (acknowledge an alarm, open a door, disable a zone) is written to the system's log with your name and the phone's model, just as when you do it at a computer.
  • Location, only for the hold-up alarm. If you have turned on the hold-up alarm yourself and hold the button for three seconds, the phone's location is sent about every 15 seconds until an operator resets the alarm. Operators see it, and it is kept in the system's log. The app never reads your location otherwise.

Through Ampano's push relay and Google

  • Notifications are encrypted by your organisation's server so that only your phone can read them (RFC 8291). Ampano's relay and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver them and see only the push address, the time and an encrypted body of the same size every time.
  • Acknowledgements from a notification and hold-up alarms can go through the relay when the phone cannot reach the server directly. They are signed by the phone, so the relay cannot change them. They are not encrypted towards the relay, so the relay can read an acknowledgement comment and, for a hold-up alarm, the location. The relay keeps them in memory until the server collects them, at most one day, and does not write them to disk.

Deletion

  • Uninstalling the app deletes its keys and settings from the phone.
  • An administrator in your organisation removes the phone from Spine (⚙ › Accounts). Your account and the log are handled under your organisation's rules; contact them to obtain or delete data about you.
